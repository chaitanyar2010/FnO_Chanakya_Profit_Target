How the calculation works

Risk per unit = |Entry − Stop-loss|. Total risk = Quantity × risk per unit. Points at each target = |Target − Entry|.

Probability-weighted reward = (Q1 × P1 × Points1) + (Q2 × P2 × Points2) + … + (Q5 × P5 × Points5). R : R = weighted reward ÷ total risk, and the plan must reach your minimum (1.5 by default).

Choosing the split. Among all splits in whole lots that meet the minimum R : R and the minimum share per target, the calculator books the most at T1, then the most at T2, then T3 and T4, with the rest at T5. This locks in profit as early as possible.

Expected P&L treats the probabilities as one chain: stop-loss before T1 (100% − P1), T1 then stopped out (P1 − P2), and so on up to T4 then stopped out (P4 − P5), and all five targets (P5). It assumes every trade ends at a target or a stop. The stop-loss setting decides what the unbooked quantity earns when stopped out. Brokerage, taxes, slippage and gaps are not included, and the probabilities are your estimates.
